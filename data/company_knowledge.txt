FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
---------------------------

Q: How long does implementation take?
A: Simple chatbot deployments take 2–3 weeks. Complex custom AI projects take 6–12 weeks depending on data availability and integration requirements.

Q: Do you provide post-deployment support?
A: Yes. All our solutions include a 30-day post-deployment warranty. We offer ongoing support packages starting at ₹8,000/month.

Q: Can your solutions integrate with existing software?
A: Absolutely. We have pre-built connectors for Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, SAP, Tally, and most REST-API-compatible systems. Custom integrations are also available.

Q: What data do you need to get started?
A: For chatbots — your existing FAQ documents and service information. For predictive analytics — 12–24 months of historical transaction data is ideal. For computer vision — labelled image datasets or access to live camera feeds.

Q: Is our data secure?
A: Yes. We follow ISO 27001 practices, encrypt data at rest and in transit, and never share client data with third parties. We can sign NDAs and data-processing agreements.

Q: Do you offer demos?
A: Yes! We offer free 30-minute product demonstrations. Contact our sales team or book through our website.

Q: Can small businesses afford AI automation?
A: Our entry-level chatbot plans start at ₹25,000/month, making AI automation accessible to SMEs. We also offer project-based pricing for one-time implementations.

Q: What ROI can we expect?
A: Clients typically report 30–60% reduction in customer support tickets within 3 months, and 20–40% improvement in lead conversion rates when using our sales assistant.
